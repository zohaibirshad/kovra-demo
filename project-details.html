The challenge

A climate-tech startup that looked like a bank

Aether Labs builds carbon-capture hardware for cities. Their science was world-class, but their brand felt cold and corporate, and their website struggled to explain what they actually do.

We were asked to create an identity and digital presence that would resonate with investors, city planners and future hires alike, in under ten weeks.

The solution

Clean air, made tangible

We built the identity around a single idea: visible air. A fluid logomark, a palette drawn from sky and stone and a motion language based on slow, breathing gradients.

“Kovra translated complex science into a brand that feels human. Our investor deck finally matches the ambition of our work.”Dr. Elena Varga, CEO, Aether Labs
